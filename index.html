Turn Your Waste Into Better Health

Recycle → Earn → Improve lives.


Trash for Health is a business in Nigeria that turns plastic trash into money for local healthcare.

The company builds a smart network to collect plastic waste and sells it to manufacturing factories.

Instead of letting plastic ruin neighborhoods, the enterprise uses its cash value to pay for basic medical care.

How It Works...

♻️ Collect → 📦 Submit → ⚖️ Verify → ❤️ Health Benefits → 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦Social Impact.

A household or business gives us recyclable waste. Our community collectors recover it.

We weigh, sort and digitally record the material. The material goes to our recycling partners.

The recycler pays for the recovered material. At the same time, corporations can pay us for verified plastic-recovery and sustainability programmes.

Our healthcare partners deliver approved services such as:

  1. 1. Health education.
  2. 2. Basic screening.
  3. 3. Maternal-health support.
  4. 4. Malaria prevention and testing.
  5. 5. Referrals.
  6. 6. Other preventive healthcare interventions.
  7. 7. And every part of the system is measured.
